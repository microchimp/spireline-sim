## Symptom
Lockstep record `FTV3HZZPFE` #135 (block `real-s2-m12`, generation 0, scored with the F08 switch off): at round 3 of the Soul Fysh fight the boss goes 191 -> 175 in the game (a Strike dealt 16) and 191 -> 177 in the emulator (14). The player holds Pen Nib with six Attacks played at the fight's start, so this Strike is the tenth Attack; the player has Strength 2 and no other damage modifier on either side; Soul Fysh has no block and no powers at that step. The week-4 trace tool (`referee/lockstep_trace.py`) replayed the run to the record (floor 17, round 3, 157 asks) and mapped the ask to the run record's fight decision 53, step 10. The synthetic setup fight could not restore the RNG, and its natural replay (Strength 0) matched on both sides at 12 = 6 x 2, which is why the 2026-10-24 probes found nothing for this group: the difference needs Strength together with the tenth Attack. The record's arithmetic has one simple fit: the game computes (6 + 2) x 2 = 16, the emulator 6 x 2 + 2 = 14 (the other fit, 7 x 2 with the emulator at Strength 1, needs the emulator to have lost a Strength the game kept; the Strikes at steps 6 and 11 dealt 8 on the game side, so the game had Strength 2 throughout).

## Expected behaviour (game)
`Hook.ModifyDamage` folds the additive terms before the multiplicative ones: `StrengthPower.ModifyDamageAdditive` adds the owner's Strength to the card's base (after the enchantment's own additive and multiplicative steps), and `PenNib.ModifyDamageMultiplicative` then doubles the total on the tenth Attack (the relic's counter rises in `PenNib.BeforeCardPlayed`, so the tenth card sees it wrapped to zero). A Strike at Strength 2 on the tenth Attack deals 16; at Strength 0, 12.

## Emulator mechanism and its other uses
- Switch-off path: `CardEffects.DmgFrom` -> `ModifiedCardDamage` applies the card-reading relic multiplier `RelicEffects.CardDamageMultiplier` (Pen Nib, and Vitruvian Minion for Minion-tagged cards) and the Gigantification multiplier to the card's own damage (base, enchantment, card relic bonuses) before the attack path in `CombatEngine` adds the creature-level additive terms (Strength and whatever else that path adds after `DmgFrom`: read it and list them). Every one of those additive terms is doubled by the game and not by the emulator on a Pen Nib tenth Attack, a Vitruvian Minion card or a Gigantified Attack; the three multipliers share the site, so one fix covers them (move the multiplicative fold after the additive creature terms, or pass the Strength-inclusive value in).
- Switch-on path (main since the F08+F10 switch-on landing, `Core2.Switch.F08`): `DmgFrom` returns the base and Core2's damage command folds the listeners in the game's order (`Core2/Models/Relics/PenNib.cs` is a fold-mul listener, `Core2/Models/Powers/StrengthPower.cs` an additive one). It is expected to give 16 already; the scenario test decides. If it does, the defect is confined to the fallback engine (`--engine` legacy replays, generation-0 blocks such as `real-s2-*`) and the kit is low priority; if it does not, the fold order in Core2's `AttackCommand` is wrong and this becomes the rebuild lane's F08 item.
