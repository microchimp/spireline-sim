# Kit G96df219a44 (rank 9, weight 66.4, family F16 / rule R17: treasure, .hp and .max_hp). Shared body for batch K1-T1 (G96df219a44, G2fc811be54, Gac0a0b8a7e)

## Symptom
At the referee's treasure question (the "take the chest relic / skip" decision) the lockstep reports player hp and max_hp higher on the emulator side by the same amount: 63/80 against 70/87, 45/90 against 55/100, 41/80 against 55/94, 58/80 against 65/87, 20/91 against 27/98 (deltas 7, 10, 14, 7, 7; hp and max_hp move together). 11 divergences in 11 runs, first floor 10, 31 decisions left unranked behind them, 4 confirmed outcome-changing (the weight comes from the unranked spans, not from a wrong game outcome). All five sample records are roots (four runs have no earlier divergence; the first lists earlier ones at decisions 14 and 38, which are other fields). The `acted` field of each record (a map step, a rest HEAL, an event option, a card reward pick) is the step BEFORE the treasure question and is not the cause: the record `type` is the kind of the decision at which the comparison failed. The deltas equal the max-HP pickup effects the emulator already models for Strawberry, Pear and Mango, and the emulator raises hp by the same amount (`RunNonCombatEffects.GainMaxHp`). This is not a simulator error: the comparison is made at a moment when the two sides are in different states by construction.

## Expected behaviour (game)
Room flow: `TreasureRoom.EnterInternal` fires the room-entered hooks and calls `TreasureRoomRelicSynchronizer.BeginRelicPicking`, which rolls the chest relic. When the player opens the chest, `OneOffSynchronizer.DoLocalTreasureRoomRewards` runs `DoTreasureRoomRewards`: the chest gold is paid (a roll on the player rewards stream, scaled down at the poverty ascension, which A10 includes), then `TryHandleSpoilsMap`. The chest relic is only obtained when the player picks it (`PickRelicLocally`, then `RelicCmd.Obtain`, which adds the relic and runs the relic own `AfterObtained`). So at the moment the referee is asked "take or skip": the chest gold is already paid, the relic is NOT yet held, and none of its pickup effects (max HP, gold, card upgrades, ...) have happened. The pickup effect shows in the referee state only at the decision after the take.

## Emulator mechanism and its other uses
- Emulator side of the lockstep: `referee/lockstep.py`, class `EmuLock`. The training engine treats the chest as no decision (`rl/run_train/engine.py::_treasure` returns an auto step, DESIGN.md 1.5.2), so by the time `EmuLock.sync("treasure", ...)` returns, the session has already walked through the chest and the C# engine has run `RunEngine.StepTreasure` -> `RunNonCombatEffects.ApplyRelicPickup` (the pickup effect is applied). `EmuLock.apply` for kind "treasure" does nothing for take (and returns "treasure_skip" for skip); `EmuLock.choose` always answers take.
- Comparison: `Lockstep._equal` (referee/lockstep.py, the `if kind == "treasure"` branch, about line 352). It adds the offered relic(s) from the referee `["take", RELIC]` labels to the referee view `relics`, which fixes only the relic list. It does not fix anything the relic pickup does, so every chest relic with a pickup effect diverges: max-HP relics (this group), card-upgrade relics (G2fc811be54: deck), Old Coin (Gac0a0b8a7e: gold).
- Other callers of `_equal`: every decision kind uses the same function, and `Lockstep._reanchor` calls `_equal` again after a re-anchor, so a change to the treasure branch also governs re-anchoring at a chest. Keep both paths correct.
- The C# engine side is right and stays unchanged: `RunRewardGenerator.EnterTreasureRoom` (relic offered on `RunState.RelicReward`, chest gold paid at entry), `RunEngine.StepTreasure` (take applies the pickup), `ApplyRelicPickup` (Strawberry, Pear, Mango, Whetstone, War Paint, Old Coin cases exist). Do not touch `Core2/` or other rebuild-lane areas.

## Fix sketch
In `Lockstep._equal`, treasure branch, after the offered relics are added to the referee `relics`: if every offered relic appears in the emulator view `relics` (the emulator holds what the referee is offered), drop from the diff the entries whose path is one a relic pickup can change: `.hp`, `.max_hp`, `.gold`, `.deck`, `.potions`. Leave `.floor`, `.relics` and everything else compared. Put the path set in one module constant next to `MAPPED_KINDS` with a comment saying why (the take has not happened on the referee side; the next decision compares these fields). The normal comparison at the next decision is the real check of the pickup effect, and that record will carry the take as its `acted`. Alternative, more faithful and larger (landing session call): have `EmuLock` stop before the auto chest step, view the emulator with the relic offered but not taken, and apply the take in `EmuLock.apply`; that needs a hold flag on `RunSession` in `rl/run_train/engine.py` and is not needed to fix these groups.
