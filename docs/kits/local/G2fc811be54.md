# Kit G2fc811be54 (rank 33, weight 35.3, family F16 / rule R17: treasure, .deck). Same mechanism as G96df219a44

Short kit: the full body (expected behaviour, emulator mechanism, fix sketch, gates) is `G96df219a44.md` in this folder; implement them together as one change (batch K1-T1).

## Symptom
At the treasure question the deck differs: on the emulator side two cards are already upgraded, on the referee side not yet. Five sample records, 6 divergences, first floor 8, 15 decisions unranked, 3 confirmed outcome-changing, all roots (no earlier divergence in any of the five). Examples: two Defends at upgrade level 1 on the emulator only; a Spite and a Strike; a Bludgeon and a Body Slam; two Strikes; a Bash and a Strike. Always exactly two cards, of one card type per record (two skills in one record, two attacks in the others). That is the pickup effect of the two card-upgrade chest relics War Paint (two skills) and Whetstone (two attacks): `RunNonCombatEffects.ApplyRelicPickup` cases `RelicWarPaint` and `RelicWhetstone` -> `UpgradeRandomDeckCards`. `acted` (a select_cards pick, a map step, a card reward pick) is the step before the question and is not the cause.

## Expected behaviour (game)
As in G96df219a44: the relic is held only after the player pick, and its `AfterObtained` upgrades the two cards then. At the question the referee deck has no upgrade from it.

## Emulator mechanism and its other uses
Same as G96df219a44 (the `Lockstep._equal` treasure branch compares the deck at a moment when the emulator has already taken the relic). Other users of `UpgradeRandomDeckCards` and the events that upgrade cards (Doors of Light and Dark use their own shuffle on the event stream) are untouched by this fix.
