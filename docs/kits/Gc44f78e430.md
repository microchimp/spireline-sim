## Expected behaviour (game)
`CombatManager.EndCombatInternal` runs `Hook.AfterCombatEnd`, then `Hook.AfterCombatVictory`, then the reward room. `BurningBlood.AfterCombatVictory` heals the owner by its heal var (6) when the owner is not dead, with no condition on how the enemies left (kill or escape). So the heal is the same for every won fight, and the emulator pays it the same way (`RunRewardGenerator.GenerateCombatRewards`, once for every won fight). A one-sided Burning Blood heal would therefore show in almost every fight; it shows in three. What differs is what happens in the enemy phase before the end. The rules this kit could not read in the allowed calls and the landing session must read first: how `DamageCmd.Attack` behaves for a multi-hit move when the attacker dies between hits (retaliation such as Flame Barrier or Thorns, `PowerModel.AfterDamageReceived`), where `CombatManager` checks the win (after each action or after the enemy's whole move), and the order of the player's end-of-turn powers against the enemy moves.
