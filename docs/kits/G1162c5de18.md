## Symptom
Two records, reason `phase:0!=rewards` with an empty diff: the referee is on the reward screen and the emulator is still in the combat (phase 0).
- EJCR9EGET5#98, floor 14. Last acted label: play CINDER (hand slot 2). Earlier divergence 78 (floor 12, PHROG_PARASITE, `.combat.enemy_hp` 4 against 14).
- TQ9EGCTTWL#50, floor 5. Last acted label: play TEAR_ASUNDER (hand slot 3). No earlier divergence.
Both cards cost 2, so the agent was left with one energy (3 energy) or none. The run block had 74 such records; commit cedb41b7 explains why: the referee asks a combat question only with two or more legal actions and takes a lone End Turn for the agent; the lockstep took that End Turn only when the next question was a combat one, so a fight that ended on it (an end-of-turn power, a retaliation in the enemy phase) was logged as `phase:0!=rewards` with the agent's last card play as the acted label.

## Expected behaviour (game)
Either the fight ended on the card play (the card killed the last enemy; then the game is on the reward screen at once) or it ended in the enemy phase after the referee's own End Turn (then the acted label shows only the last agent action). The first needs the emulator to deal less damage than the game or to miss the kill; the second is the artefact.

## Emulator mechanism and its other uses
Answer to the question in the brief: both records are the artefact's residue until a re-run says otherwise. The acted label is a card play, which is what the old sync logged when the End Turn that ended the fight was not the agent's; the emulator showed no earlier enemy difference in TQ9EGCTTWL (no earlier divergence at all), so the card play itself did not leave the sides apart before the End Turn. These two probably come from the block run before cedb41b7 reached the lockstep that produced the ranking.
If one survives a re-run with the current lockstep, the candidate is Tear Asunder: in the game `TearAsunder` hits `1 + (the number of DamageReceivedEntry in the combat history whose receiver is the player and whose unblocked damage is above zero)` times (the `CalculatedHits` var). In the emulator `CardEffects` (case `IC.TearAsunder`, near line 682) uses `1 + CombatState.UnblockedDamageHitCount`, and that counter is incremented in one place only: the enemy attack path in `EnemyAI.cs` (near line 4478). Self-inflicted HP loss (Hemokinesis, Bloodletting, Offering, Brand-like cards), retaliation damage and poison on the player never count, although in the game they are damage received if they go through the damage command. A Tear Asunder with fewer hits than the game's leaves the enemy alive: the emulator stays in the combat while the game shows rewards. That is exactly this symptom. (The Cinder record has no such counter: Cinder is a flat 18 or 24 followed by `CombatCardSelection` picking a card of the hand to exhaust, which matches the emulator's comment.)
Other users of the counter: `StateCloning.cs` (copies it), `CombatSnapshot.cs` key `unblocked_damage_hit_count`, and `LavaLamp`'s own tally (a separate field; the comment near `CardEffects.cs` line 4089 says self-inflicted HP loss is damage received for it).

## Fix sketch
Only for step 2. Increment `UnblockedDamageHitCount` wherever the player takes unblocked damage as a damage instance in the game, not only in the monster attack path: the single HP-loss step `Effects.CardEffects.LosePlayerHp` is the common point (every damage instance goes through it, per the comment in `EnemyAI.cs`), so count there when the amount that remains after Intangible, Tungsten Rod and Buffer is above zero, and drop the increment in `EnemyAI.cs` so a hit is counted once. First read the game's `CreatureCmd.Damage` and `CreatureCmd.LoseHp` to see which of the two writes a `DamageReceivedEntry` (poison and direct HP loss may not); count only the ones that do.
