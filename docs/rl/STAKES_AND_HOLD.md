# Stakes schedule, potion hold and sampled imitation (2026-11-06)

Branch `feat/stakes-hold-sampling` (fork main `58aaa6fe` plus this). Written for milestone 7 and milestone 6 of the 2026-11-05 strategy memo (D55; `docs/design/2026-11-05-strategy-rethink.md` sections 2.2 causes 1 and 3, 3.5 step 4, 5.1), from the play-or-deck measurement (`docs/diagnostics/2026-11-05-play-or-deck.md`: sixteen times the simulations lifts the boss win rate by about 10.6 points at both bosses on the humans' own starts, four times by 4 to 6; at the Act 2 boss half the gap to humans is the hand brought to it, chiefly potions 1.68 against 0.48 at the door) and the imitation chooser's sanity run (`docs/rl/IMITATION_CHOOSER.md`: the bare argmax of the prior skips Act 1 card rewards 5.4 times in 100 against the humans' 15.4).

## 2. How the schedule reaches every player

`agent.json` gains three optional keys (absent in every earlier candidate, whose behaviour and recipe are unchanged):

```
"search_schedule": {"base": "a3-v1", "mult": {"hall": 1, "elite": 4, "boss": 16}, "tag": "a3-v1@h1e4b16"},
"potion_hold": {"hp_frac": 0.3, "fights": "hall"},
"recipe": "vrun+stakes:a3-v1@h1e4b16+hold:30"
```

* **The config.** `rl/search_configs.py`: a `SearchConfig` has `stakes` (hall, elite, boss multipliers) and `potion_hold`; `Position.fight` ("hall", "elite", "boss", read by `position_of` from the run's node type); `sims_for` multiplies the base count (and the clamp's top, and, in `search_kwargs`, the safety clock) before the kind multiplier, so a boss fight is exactly the play-or-deck `x16` arm (a test compares every fight kind and decision kind with `fight_gym.arm_settings`) and a hallway fight is `a3-v1`. A config name carries both: `get("a3-v1@h1e4b16+hold30")`, so every consumer of a config name can play one.
* **The agent.** `gen_loop.Agent` has `stakes` and `hold` (read by `load_agent` from `search_schedule` and `potion_hold`, validated: a base that is a search config, whole multipliers 1 to 64, a whole per cent, `recipe` equal to the one the fields give). `Agent.search_config(sims)` is the one place that decides what the agent searches with: `sims` as the lab passes it (`--config a3-v1`), unless the agent has a schedule, whose own base replaces it. A plain agent gets exactly `search_configs.get(sims)` as before. `describe()` names `stakes` and `hold` only where set.
* **The emulator arm** (the promotion's two arms in `gen_loop.promote` / `run_arm`, the funnel's looks, the night's copy arm, `gen0.py emu` self-play and the lab's filler, the sanity runs): `gen0.play_emu_run` asks `agent.search_config(sims)` and `gen0.run_block` the same for the shards' config; every one passes the agent it plays. `run_arm`'s arm cache and `gen0.emu_arm_match` compare `describe()` (which now carries the schedule and the hold), so an arm played at another schedule is never reused. The summary keeps the lab's `config` (so the pairing check's config comparison still reads equal in both arms) and adds `search_config` (`a3-v1@h1e4b16+hold30`); a run row's `config` is the effective name and, under a hold, carries `hold_judged` and `hold_dropped`.
* **The real-game arm** (the night, `gen0.py score`): `referee/lockstep.py` `Player` asks `lockstep.search_config(sims)`, which loads the agent `SPIRELINE_GEN_DIR` names and asks the same `Agent.search_config`, and reads the fight's position whenever the config needs it (`needs_position`, which a schedule, a stakes multiplier or a hold all make true). `rl/nightly_real.py` needs no change: it passes `--gen-dir` and `--config` to `gen0.py score`, which does both arms.
* **The attempt count.** `gen_loop.candidate_recipe(agent)` is `Agent.recipe()` = `macro_recipe.full_recipe(macro, stakes, hold)`: the macro's recipe (the blended string, "" for plain `vrun`), then `+stakes:<tag>` and `+hold:<per cent>`; an agent with neither is the recipe it always was. `promotions.tsv`'s `macro` column, `attempts_before`, `attempts_spent`, `block_used_by_another`, `resolve_e_block` and `gen0.py train`'s cap check (now `candidate_recipe(load_agent(built))`) count per recipe: the stakes candidate and the stakes-and-hold candidate each start at attempt 1 with a cap of two, each on a block no other recipe used, and generation 12's closed self-play line (two refusals) counts against neither. The pick mode of the chooser (section 4) is part of the macro string, so it is part of the recipe too.
* **Wall limits.** A stakes agent's boss fights think up to 16 times as long, so the plain per-run limits are multiplied by `Agent.wall_factor()` = largest multiplier over four (4 for `h1e4b16`; a plain agent 1): the emulator run's in-run cap (`RUN_WALL_CAP_S` 1,800 s), the dispatcher's kill (`RUN_TIMEOUT_S` 600 s) and the real arm's `--run-deadline-s` (`REAL_RUN_DEADLINE_S` 1,800 s, equal to `referee/envproto.py`'s; a test keeps them equal). The step timeouts of the real arm (180 s) are the game's reply to an action, not the agent's thinking, and are unchanged.

## 3. Expected block cost

The a3-v1 arm of the 2026-11-03 to 11-05 nights (2,699 finished copy runs, 12 processes, the lab's load): mean run 29.4 s, median 26.1, 99th 82.6, longest 147 s; a 200-run arm took 569 s. A regression of a run's wall on the fights it played (monsters 6.9, elites 0.8, bosses 1.2 a run) puts about 70% of the search on hallway fights, 8% on elites and 22% on bosses; the pilot of the play-or-deck run timed a3-v1 boss fights at 5.3 s (Act 1) and 9.0 s (Act 2) and x16 at 78.5 and 144.5 s (about 15 times), x4 at 20.6 and 43.5 s (about 4.5 times), and its full run's means (6.4 s a3-v1, 74 s x16 at the Act 1 boss) give 11.6 times. So at hallway x1, elite x4 and boss x16 a run costs

* about **5 times** the a3-v1 run (from 3.7 times with the full-run ratios, 11.6 and 3, to 6.8 times with the pilot's, 16 and 4): a mean run of about 150 s (110 to 200), the slowest runs near 600 to 800 s;
* a 2,000-seed block's candidate arm about **80 core-hours** (60 to 115) against 16 for an a3-v1 arm: about 5 hours on 16 processes (3.8 to 7); the incumbent's arm is the usual 16 core-hours (an hour), or free where the funnel reuses it. The funnel's 200-seed smoke is about 8 core-hours (half an hour on 16 processes), which is where a refusal usually comes.
* A boss fight in a 2,000-seed block is about 16 times longer than before; elites about 4 times; the hallway not at all. A block that reaches the Act 2 boss spends most of its time there.
* The hold adds, at a hallway decision with a potion held beside other moves, one kill check (`rl/lethal.py`, at most 50 ms; 200 sequences or 240 plays, whichever comes first) when the config's own kill check is off, which `a3-v1` leaves it. Not measured; see section 6.
* The nightly's per-agent `--stop-after-h 4` is the real arm's hours: a stakes agent promoted to incumbent would play a 500-seed night in about 2 hours on the real arm plus the copy arm's 200 runs (about 40 minutes), inside it; a 2,000-seed night (a promotion day) would not be. That is the lab's to set at promotion; nothing here changes `jobs.json`.

## 4. The hold rule, in one sentence

In a hallway fight (not an elite or a boss) the root's potion moves are dropped from the search's candidates unless the root's kill check finds no verified kill this turn **and** the HP left after the enemies' announced attacks, less the block the player holds now, would be under 30 in 100 of max HP.

`live_search.search_live(potion_hold=<fraction>)` and `search_steps` do it (`_Search.hold_potions`, with `hold_keeps_potions` as the pure rule): the kill check is the search's own when `lethal_check` ran (a verified kill answers the decision and the hold is never reached), else `lethal.find_lethal` is run once; the position is read off the faithful copy (`combat_mcts.incoming_damage`, the repo's "attack damage announced against the player this turn"); a check that cannot run keeps the potions (the hold only removes moves on a positive reading); a root with no potion, or with nothing but potions, is not judged. Block that this turn's cards would add is not counted, so the rule keeps a potion in reach somewhat more often than the position needs. The rollouts below the root can still use a potion; only the root's choice is held. `SearchResult.hold` records the judgement ({"kept", "killed", "hp", "after", "threshold", "incoming", "block", "potions"}). `SearchConfig.search_kwargs` passes `potion_hold` in hallway fights only; elites and bosses are untouched. The threshold is the recipe's (`hold:30`): another threshold is another recipe.

## 5. The sampled chooser

`imitation_chooser.BlendedPolicy` takes a `pick` mode, part of the macro string (`macro_recipe.PICKS`; the argmax names nothing, so `candidate-imit` and `candidate-imit-l05` keep their recipes):

* **`sample`** (`:pick=sample`): the option is drawn from softmax((V + lambda log pi_h) / tau) at tau 1 over the options the guard leaves (a card reward, an ancient, the smith's target; the rest site on its flat joint score, as exploring self-play samples it). The stream is seeded by the run's game seed, the floor, the screen kind and the screen's count on that floor (`begin_run(seed)`, called by `gen0.play_emu_run` and the lockstep `Player`), never by what any other decision consumed, so a block replays exactly and the copy arm and the real arm (same seed, same floors) draw alike. A policy that was not given `begin_run` refuses to sample. Exploring self-play (`--explore-tau`) overrides tau as before.
* **`skip-calibrated`** (`:pick=skipcal<bias>`): the argmax of the blend with `bias` floors added to the skip option's score on card rewards. `rl/skip_calibrate.py` finds the bias: it plays `--runs` (50) runs of the chooser on the copy (plain `a3-v1` search; `--keep-search` for the agent's own), reads the Act 1 skip rate off each row's `cards`, and, if it is not within 3 points of the humans' 15.4, takes the bias that would have given the target on the skip gaps of the screens that round met (every `skipcal` row's `decided` carries each Act 1 card screen's gap: the bias at which the skip would have been taken; `imitation_chooser.skip_gap`), and plays again at that bias, up to four rounds: the rate at a bias is the share of gaps at or under it, but the screens move with the bias, so only a round played at the final bias landing in the tolerance counts (`landed`). 20 to 40 minutes a round on one process: hold a QUIET.lock line (`--quiet-tag`), at most 2 processes.

```
python rl/skip_calibrate.py --agent-dir <agent>/gen12/<any built blended candidate> --lib <native out dir> --out <dir> --runs 50 --procs 2
```

The bias goes into `gen0.py build ... --pick skipcal --skip-bias <bias>`. The calibration is the chooser's bias on the plain search; a candidate that also plays the stakes and the hold changes the fights' prices, not the Act 1 card screens' options, but its skip rate is not re-measured here.
