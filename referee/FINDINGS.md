Once the colony had lost its 20 in a turn, further damage that turn landed in full, and the readout showed the remaining cap (14 where the game shows 20, the display half, H01). Root cause: `HardenedShellPower` keeps `Amount` (20) all fight and counts the owner's lost HP in a private `damageReceivedThisTurn`; `ModifyHpLostBeforeOstyLate` returns `Math.Min(amount, Amount - damageReceivedThisTurn)` (nothing for 0), `AfterDamageReceived` adds `result.UnblockedDamage` (the HP `LoseHpInternal` really took: after block and every modifier, clamped at the target's HP, none for a fully blocked hit), `BeforeSideTurnStart` zeroes it for EITHER side's turn. The emulator subtracted the HP lost from the buff and `BuffSystem` dropped it at 0, so `hardened > 0` was false and nothing capped until `RefillHardenedShells` put it back.
